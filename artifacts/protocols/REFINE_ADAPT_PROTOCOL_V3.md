# Subject-held-out head adaptation

The class-stratified head fit improved the official test but reduced accuracy on the held-out-subject validation split. This is an exploratory diagnostic of domain shift, not a test-driven hyperparameter search. Keep the frozen stage-3 resource controller, Backbone and High convolutions, feasible training masks, seeds 711–719, optimizer, training limit, and cross-entropy selection metric unchanged. Replace only the head-training partition: choose four entire fit subjects with RNG seed 2026 as an internal selection set; train the correction head on all remaining fit subjects. Every class must appear on both sides. Save the subject identities and per-class counts. Select the epoch by mean full-recording cross-entropy across traditional sharing, predictive fixed-reserve sharing, and PIRC. Freeze the checkpoint before any validation or official-test replay; evaluate both splits once and report all outcomes, including the earlier class-stratified model and failures. Prior exposure of both external splits means this remains exploratory.
