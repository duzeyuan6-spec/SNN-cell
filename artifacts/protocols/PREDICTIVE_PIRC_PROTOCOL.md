# Predictive PIRC online experiment protocol

Frozen before validation evaluation. Existing DVS128 Gesture seed-42 checkpoint and its validation logit/activity cache are unchanged. No official test labels or model retraining. Train a light causal 4-step per-group request predictor on the 924 fitting clips only, with current and past local firing rates and request EMAs. Its target is future request rate; the predictor sees no future validation samples or supply.

Primary condition: local capacity 4, mean supply 1.6, 64-step outage and supply CV 0.5 (`d64_v50`), 64 held-out validation clips and exogenous seeds 711–713. Secondary: all six original outage-duration × volatility cases at capacity 4, plus capacity 12 anchor, same seeds and clips. The supply and original top-two High requests are fixed and paired across methods. Four-step allocation delay, same initial reserve 2 and local capacity, no instantaneous transfer. All original requests count in RCIR, including any intentionally deferred calls. High MACs are charged only for served calls. Network logits are evaluated by validated cached sparse corrections; physical network replay on the primary case is an audit.

Three online methods: fit-calibrated reserve threshold, predictive threshold using 4-step demand forecast and reserve, and predictive PIRC adding a slow local supply-demand error/debt state. All policy weights selected on the first 128 fitting clips, seeds 701–703, and predictor parameters fitted on all 924 fitting clips only, with a prespecified 0.5 overall RCIR + 0.5 outage RCIR + 0.1 spill fraction objective. Equal primary supply budget; report overall RCIR, outage service, spill, calls, MACs, and classification accuracy. Keep all fitting candidates and validation cases. The prior omniscient offline bound is contextual only and never compared as an online method.

Candidate grids: threshold `gain ∈ {0,2,4,8}`, `boost ∈ {2,4,8}`; predictive threshold adds forecast gain `{0,1,2,4,8}` to the best threshold; PIRC adds the same forecast term with `rho ∈ {0.5,0.9,0.98}`, slow error gain `{1,2,4}`, debt gain fixed at 1. Predictor horizon is four bins to match supply allocation delay. Ties favor simpler/smaller parameters. Fit objective is equal mean across three fit seeds of the primary condition. No validation-label-based parameter selection.
