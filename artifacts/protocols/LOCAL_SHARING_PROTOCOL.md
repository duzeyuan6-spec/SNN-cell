# Local sharing and cross-region starvation diagnostic

Frozen before validation results. DVS128 Gesture seed-42 checkpoint, original top-two High requests, fitting/validation rate caches, four-step supply allocation delay, and exogenous supply traces remain unchanged. No SNN retraining or official test labels. Fit policy parameters on 128 fitting clips and seeds 701–703. Primary validation: first 64 held-out validation clips, seeds 711–713, capacity 4, mean supply 1.6, `d64_v50`. Secondary: all six outage-duration × volatility scenarios at capacities 4 and 12. Report all cases.

All three policies share the same causal reserve-threshold supply-allocation rule (`gain=4, boost=2`), initial reserve 2, and total exogenous supply. The allocator may change its regional split in response to the resulting reserves; exogenous supply is identical. A 2×2 patch adjacency graph has edges (0,1), (0,2), (1,3), (2,3). Sharing occurs after the current delayed supply arrives, before current High calls. It is conservative 1:1 token transfer, at most 1 token total per time bin and at most 1 per edge, with no cross-region instantaneous pooling. Donors retain enough for their own current High call. Traditional threshold uses no transfer. Simple balancing serves the smallest currently rescuable deficit first. Predictive PIRC uses causal local activity, fitting-only four-step demand predictor, and EMA of unmet local request pressure to choose recipients and donors. All original requests remain in RCIR denominator, and only served calls incur High MACs.

Global CRSR: missed High calls at a bin with another region retaining resource after its own call, divided by all original calls. Actionable local CRSR: missed calls with an adjacent donor holding enough transferable surplus after its own call; a separate indicator uses the available one-token transfer budget. Report both with RCIR so depletion cannot masquerade as improved coordination. Group demand, incoming supply, end reserve, spill and service are logged. PIRC parameters selected only on fitting clips by the prespecified objective `0.5 RCIR + 0.5 outage RCIR + 0.1 spill_fraction`; tie favors smaller weights. Any additional sensitivity after validation is labeled exploratory. Equal supply budget and transfer constraints are audited for every paired trace.

Measurement clarification recorded after the first audit: donor surplus is `max(0, pre-service available resources minus the donor's own current requested token)`. A failed donor request with a fractional residual is not considered surplus. The first diagnostic run used the wrong CRSR denominator eligibility and is retained under `results/local_sharing_seed42/audit_bad_surplus_v1/`; the allocation policy and its calibration objective were unaffected, but all CRSR values are recomputed in the final run.
