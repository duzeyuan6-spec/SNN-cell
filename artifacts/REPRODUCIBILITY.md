## Fixed seven-method rules and PIRC selection

The seven methods are **project control rules**, not published algorithms from seven external papers. The first three perform no sharing: Fixed Allocation routes supply uniformly; Instant Gating uses `softmax(4 × current local spike activity)`; EMA Gating uses `softmax(4 × EMA_0.9(activity))`. None reduces the original request denominator. Reserve Threshold uses the traditional request/reserve allocator and fixed one-token donor safety; Balanced Sharing uses the same allocator with zero donor safety; Predictive Reserve retains fixed one-token safety and the causal four-bin demand forecast. PIRC uses the adaptive donor safety, slow local demand/supply state and delayed projected-reserve allocation in `local_pirc/unified_system.py`. All sharing methods use the same 2×2 adjacency graph and one-token-per-bin transfer limit. The exact locked coefficients are in `configs/resource_controller.json` and the seven-method manifest.

The historical PIRC controller search is now fully archived under [`controller_selection/`](results/controller_selection/): Stage 1 searched 36 safety/forecast combinations; Stage 2 searched 48 burst-protection combinations; both had zero jointly feasible candidates. Stage 3 searched 27 projected-reserve combinations and selected `safe_floor=0.5`, `safe_gap_gain=0.5`, `safe_forecast_gain=1`, `burst_gain=2`, `lookahead_gain=2`, `alloc_forecast_gain=1`, `fast_gain=0`, `transfer_priority_gain=0` by fit-only Accuracy/RCIR/outage-service feasibility and normalized improvement. **Selection used the first 256 fit clips in class order, covering only four classes.** The CSVs expose every candidate; this selection is not an independent class-balanced estimate. The seven-method table locked this controller and did no new grid search. The methods are not equally hyperparameter-optimized.

The unified-system ablations used a **different, preset controller** on the earlier checkpoint. There was no tuning grid. Removing reserve feedback deletes the reserve allocation and adaptive donor-safety terms; removing activity history replaces the full causal predictor with a current-only ridge predictor; removing sharing disables adjacent transfers. Its numerical results must not be combined with the later seven-method or Compact High rows.

## Other parameter selection and limits

- **Local sharing:** fit grid `risk_gain ∈ {0,1,2}`, `slow_gain ∈ {0.5,1,2}`, `protect ∈ {0,0.25,0.5}` (27 candidates), fit clips 128 and seeds 701–703. The saved choice was `(0,0.5,0)` under `0.5 RCIR + 0.5 outage RCIR + 0.1 spill fraction`. The direct simple-balancing comparator can lower overall RCIR while reducing outage service. `validation_results.csv` preserves every scenario and seed. The archived CRSR uses donor surplus **after** reserving the donor's own current request; an earlier incorrect CRSR audit is excluded.
- **Predictive online:** a ridge predictor with seven causal current/EMA activity-request features forecasts four-bin demand, fitted on 924 fit clips and seeds 701–703. The reserve threshold grid was `gain ∈ {0,2,4,8}` × `boost ∈ {2,4,8}`; forecast gain was `{0,1,2,4,8}`; predictive PIRC also searched `rho ∈ {0.5,0.9,0.98}` × slow gain `{1,2,4}`. Fit clips 128 and seeds 701–703, objective `0.5 RCIR + 0.5 outage RCIR + 0.1 spill`. Saved choices: threshold `(4,2)`, predictive threshold forecast gain `0`, predictive PIRC forecast gain `0`, `rho=0.5`, slow gain `2`. Forced nonzero-forecast and slow-feedback variants in `summary.csv` are separately labeled exploratory; their chosen configs and per-trace rows are archived. There was no official-test run.
- **Activity-history risk:** risk horizons `{16,32}`; reserve-only and reserve + current/4-bin EMA/16-bin EMA/trend L2 logistic models; `risk_gain ∈ {0,1,2,4}`. Both deployment variants selected gain `1` using fit traces, not validation labels. `models.json` contains every coefficient. The 16-bin AUPRCs were 0.74500 (reserve only) and 0.76671 (with activity history); the 32-bin AUPRCs were 0.82677 and 0.82873. A separate current-rate-only analysis is post hoc and archived as `exploratory_current_only*`. Future-shortage targets may cross independent 16-bin clips, and improved prediction did not establish improved closed-loop outage service.
- **Frozen-head adaptation:** Backbone and High convolutions stayed fixed; only `refine.head.weight` and `.bias` changed. Four whole fit subjects (`02, 07, 13, 14`) served as internal selection; epoch 2 was selected by mean full-recording cross entropy over three feasible schedulers. No hyperparameter grid was used at this stage. The configuration, positions, checkpoint and external-split results are archived. Both external splits had been viewed historically.
- **Compact High:** AdamW, learning rate 0.001, weight decay 0.001, batch 64, at most 100 epochs, patience 15, seed 42. Fit masks sample Fixed Allocation, Balanced Sharing, Predictive Reserve and PIRC at fit supply seeds 701–702. Epoch 43 minimized mean validation cross entropy across those four mask methods and validation seeds 1201–1202. There was no separate hyperparameter grid. The mixed-head table is not an isolated scheduler comparison, and its single-pass GPU timings are not a latency ranking.
- **Two recent adapters:** their exact parameter grid, selected values, equations, per-seed results and limitations are in the [separate report](RECENT_ONLINE_SCHEDULER_REPORT.md). They adapt selected online decision mechanisms to the SNN token action space; they do not reproduce all functions of the source papers.

## Metrics and reproduction

RCIR = interrupted original High requests / all original High requests. Outage service = served original requests in zero-supply bins / original requests in those bins. Executed network MACs = always-on Backbone MACs + MACs of **actually served** High calls. MACs do not measure GPU latency and generally exclude CPU controller arithmetic. CRSR, overflow, High calls, per-seed accuracy and paired differences remain in the linked CSVs. All numerical outputs above are archived results; this update did not retrain or rerun the experiments.
